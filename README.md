# Nuclear-Reactor-Simulator
This is a simulator that showcases the various workings of a nuclear reactor. Nuclear reactors are incredibly energy efficient, yet the stigma around nuclear reactors due to the various historical incidents paints nuclear reactors a bad picture. However, this game showcases the working of nuclear reactors through interactive controls. 

## Controls:
  The game features only two buttons, the left and right mouse clicks. The only other input action required is moving the mouse. Hence, the only input device required here is a mouse, making this game incredibly accessible and user-friendly.

## The levels
  The first level involves a showcase of the workings of a nuclear reactor and the fuel rods that are lowered and raised to control the process of fission. The particles showcase steam being generated. When the player moves to the next level, they can see the same steam now moving turbines, which is how nuclear reactors generate their electricity. Finally, the third level showcases the generation of steam from the nuclear power plant. 

This game was showcased alongside a live presentation and explanation of the concepts at NAFL's LabsAtPlay event, alongside my teammate.
